Geospatial Data Carpentry for Urbanism with R

This package is meant to help you learn how to apply geospatial analysis in the urban context using RStudio as an open-source and powerful software for automation, rescalability and reproducibility.

The online materials of this instruction package is meant to help you working with R from scratch on your own, up to an elementary stage where you can perform first steps of major analytical methods and tools on a spatial context for your professional/academic work or for your own learning.

The package is a combination of online structured teachings from the Carpentries Incubator and recorded video materials from the previous workshop that was held in-person at TU Delft in 2024.
